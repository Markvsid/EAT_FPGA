# Paper figures

Add original figures here with descriptive, stable filenames. Prefer vector PDF
for diagrams/plots and a reproducible script/source outside the Overleaf export.
Every LaTeX figure needs a caption, a label, and `\Description{...}` describing
its information for accessibility. Verify legibility in grayscale and at the
ACM small-format width. Do not add a board energy plot until measurements exist.

Planned figures: actual architecture and bank/dataflow schedule; operator demand;
accuracy versus measured energy; controlled ablation results; sequence sensitivity;
and a verified utilization analysis. Existing software plots remain elsewhere in
the research repository and must retain their original experimental scope.
