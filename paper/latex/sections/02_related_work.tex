\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}
DynamicViT studies dynamic token sparsification \cite{arxiv210602034}; EViT,
Token Merging, and Token Fusion establish related token reorganization and
aggregation approaches \cite{arxiv220207800,arxiv221009461,arxiv231201026}.
FlashAttention motivates attention dataflows that avoid materializing the
complete score matrix \cite{arxiv220514135}. These ideas must be distinguished
from the fixed-point arithmetic, short-sequence regime, and physical resource
constraints of the present prototype.

FPGA co-design has combined static and dynamic pruning
\cite{arxiv240314047}; the starting bibliography also includes a runtime-adaptive
FPGA inference engine \cite{arxiv260322867}. These records are starting sources,
not a completed current literature or patent audit.
