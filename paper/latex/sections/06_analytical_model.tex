\section{Analytical Performance and Resource Model}
\label{sec:model}
The existing schedule models array tails, GEMM startup, requantization,
nonlinear operators, division, selection, compaction, and memory assumptions.
It is not calibrated to the current serialized RTL services. For HAR, archived
core-bench cycles are approximately 17 times the original estimates; loaded
peripheral kernel cycles are approximately 32 times the estimates. The
comparison uses explicit, different simulation boundaries and establishes no
achieved frequency, seconds, or energy.

\writingtask{architecture lead}{Model actual requests, response latency,
overlap, bank bandwidth, and divider/control costs. Validate predictions on
held-out configurations against the research plan's approximately 10--15\%
tolerance. Preserve the original estimates and negative results. Do not claim
calibration from fitting a constant to these examples.}
