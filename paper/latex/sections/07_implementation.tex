\section{Implementation and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:implementation}
The target board is the AMD SK-KV260-G Kria KV260 Vision AI Starter Kit, with
Vivado part \texttt{xck26-sfvc784-2LV-c}. Functional analysis, elaboration, and
GHDL synthesis-front-end checks have been recorded, including a complete
platform netlist. These checks do not establish Vivado mapping, routed timing,
or FPGA execution. No board bitstream or deployed host driver is claimed here.

The current XDC proposes 6.667 ns, approximately 150 MHz. The controlling plan
calls for beginning near 200 MHz and recording any relaxation; its G3 target
is at least about 150 MHz with an explicit fallback. Both are targets rather
than measured Fmax. The current hosted VHDL CI also has documented simulator
installation failures that must be repaired before claiming a green workflow.
