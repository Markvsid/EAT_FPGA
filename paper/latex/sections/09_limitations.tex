\section{Limitations and Discussion}
\label{sec:limitations}
Current evidence establishes software numerical agreement and selected
functional RTL behavior, not FPGA energy efficiency. Physical resources,
timing, board correctness, measured power, and a calibrated performance model
remain unresolved. Strong speech noise and missing HAR gyro channels cause
substantial losses in the saved exploratory robustness study.

The scope is one device, short fixed sequences, and resident weights. It cannot
support a headline DDR weight-traffic reduction claim. Independent and shared
families have different training budgets; observed accuracy differences do not
isolate sharing. Existing token fusion/pruning work limits any standalone
novelty claim for summaries. Negative and inconclusive results must remain in
the final evaluation.
